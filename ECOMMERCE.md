### E-commerce MVP
- A solution to handle discounts, catalog management, customer orders, delivery and payments

## Requirements
- Email service e.g Mailgun
- SMS service e.g twilio
- Image Object Storage e.g AWS Storage
- Currency conversion rate

## Timeline
- It will take 2 months for both backend and frontend

## Features
- Catalog management
- Orders
- Discount and Promos
- Payment processing
- Delivery
- Notifications and newsletter
